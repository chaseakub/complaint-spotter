**Name:** Replio Complaint Spotter
**Category:** Productivity
**Summary (132 max):** Count which complaints repeat in restaurant reviews on Google Maps and Yelp, at every star rating. Nothing is uploaded.

**Description:**
See which complaints keep coming back in a restaurant's reviews.

Open a restaurant's reviews on Google Maps or Yelp, scroll to load them, and click Complaint Spotter. It counts how many reviews mention each complaint theme: wrong or missing items, slow service, cold food, rude service, cleanliness, allergy or food safety concerns, and delivery problems.

It reads every star rating, not just the bad ones. In a 2026 Replio case study of 2,365 reviews, about 1 in 5 reviews that named a complaint still gave four or five stars.

You can also highlight reviews on any page, or paste them in, one per line.

Private by design: everything runs in your browser. Reviews are never uploaded, stored or tracked. The extension only reads a page after you click it.

What it is not: it counts English phrases about food and drink. It reads only the reviews loaded on your screen, not a business's full history. Themes overlap, and a review with no match is not necessarily positive. Check the original reviews before acting on a count.

Built by Replio, which helps restaurant teams give each repeat complaint an owner, a due date and a check.

**Single purpose:** Count complaint themes in the reviews on the current page.
